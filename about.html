About Parachute

Share with someone nearby.

Parachute is a nearby file-sharing website with a playful rocket and parachute scene. Send files or a website link to one person at a time, and collect what arrives in Received.

How it works

  1. Connect. Enter your name on each device and allow microphone and location access. Parachute compares room sound to find nearby devices.
  2. Find nearby people. Check the nearby list. Everyone currently on it gets their own parachute when you send.
  3. Send an offer. Pick files or enter a website link. Each person puts their parachute in their box to receive it. Missed offers expire.
  4. Receive and save. Files and links pass through the sharing service. The recipient can save files or open links from Received after the parachute enters the box and the transfer finishes.

Received items stay in the current browser tab until they are removed or the page is refreshed. Save files you want to keep.
